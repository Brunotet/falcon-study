## 8c. Entry types in detail, 90% rule, long-term holds (condensed lessons)
**Multi-touch (S1E3):** a structure needs a **minimum of two touches**; three touches is the highest probability. The **second touch can be traded** (risk entry); if price comes back for a third touch the pattern's probability goes up. Do not insist on seeing the third touch before taking it.

**Risk vs reduced-risk entry (foundation ep. 3 pt 2):**
- Risk entry = inside the pattern, nothing confirmed yet, bigger reward, tighter stop (about 15-30 pips). Reduced-risk entry = after confirmation (a break/flag), slightly larger stop, less reward.
- On a very large pattern do not put the stop above the whole structure (huge stops); instead trade the smaller tight flag with a reduced-risk entry, and use a risk entry on a slightly larger flag.
- A retrace-candle entry typically uses about a 25 pip stop and targets the 90% rule as a minimum. Never use a stop under 15 pips.
- If about **80% of the candles are low-momentum corrective**, it is a correction; one impulse candle does not cancel it.
- There can be **many entries along one impulsive leg** (risk, reduced risk, repeated). Do not halve your stop just to hit a profit number, and do not set imaginary profit targets; use structure to squeeze the trade.
- **15m refinement (S3E3, "go-to setup"):** when a 1H flag looks excellent, refine the entry on the 15m (a flat correction of at least **six 15m candles**). Always keep the higher-timeframe structure as the main focus.
- Typical examples: risk entry at the top of a structure, three-touch channel with a retrace candle, a bull flag with a third touch at the bottom, ascending within ascending at the top (30-40 pip stop).

**90% rule (foundation ep. 3 pt 3, long-term holds):**
- It **activates only when a structure breaks impulsively** (a solid impulsive close, e.g. on the daily or weekly), not correctively. It works both ways.
- Once active: about a 90% probability that price fills about 90% of the correction, i.e. gets to roughly the start of the correction (usually fills 100%).
- Use it as the minimum target; beyond it longer-term targets exist.

**Long-term holds (S2 long-term holds):** at the top of a clean structure, a 30 pip stop risk entry with a more relaxed management; expect pullbacks of 10-15% on a trade whose full target can be 40-60%. Leave one trade running for the big move while also trading the 1H/15m moves inside it.

**Phase (S1E5):** you usually do not know you are in a corrective phase until about halfway in. If a "correction" retraces almost to the low where you first looked to buy, it was a correction. Within corrections there are impulses, and within impulses there are corrections.

## Risk addendum (foundation ep. 4)
- **Minimum reward-to-risk of 3:1** is the stated goal; risk always capped at 1%. Winners can exceed 10% on a 1% risk.
- Do not force trades near month end to reach a monthly number. Skip low-quality valid trades with little room.
- Keep about 80% high-probability setups and 20% valid trades; too many valid trades in a corrective period makes it harder.

# Falcon strategy: rulebook (DRAFT v1)

## 1. Core idea
Trendline and pattern trading on price structure. Structure first; candle shapes are only a secondary "override" (S1E4). The edge is mass psychology: price breaks a previous high, traps people on the wrong side, then drops back into the pattern (mirror for lows).

## 2. Timeframes
- Higher timeframe (daily, 4H): identify the pattern (e.g. ascending channel) and what phase price is in.
- 1H: entries and management. Always drill down from the higher timeframe.

## 3. Structure
- A valid trendline needs multi-touch identification: touches 1, 2, 3. A line needs a **second touch where price moved off it** before it counts (S2E1). A flag likewise needs its second point.
- Look left: mark previous highs/lows as likely turning areas. Best sells are at the top of a pattern, ideally **after the previous high has been broken** (higher probability).
- A trade that has not broken the previous high can still trickle higher first. It is valid but lower probability (the "hover").

## 4. Phase
- **Impulsive phase** (high already broken, first impulse back down confirmed): flags/setups here are high probability.
- **Corrective phase** (inside the pattern, high not broken): same-looking setups are lower probability and may evolve higher. Be ready to evolve the pattern.

## 5. Entry types
- **Risk entry:** anticipation entry at the top of the pattern, within the area, once price shows a rejection or low-momentum corrective candles (S2E1). Nothing is confirmed yet.
- **Reduced-risk entry:** after confirmation, e.g. a Falcon flag (impulse, correction, impulse, retrace) in the impulsive phase. Highest probability.
- Skip "average setups": trades not at the top of a clear pattern without solid multi-touch confirmation.
- Skip a setup if you cannot monitor it (S2E1).

## 6. 1H retrace candle (S2E2)
- Impulse candle, then a candle that retraces it. The retrace may be larger or smaller than the impulse; valid if it retraces **more than 50%**. It need not be the very next candle.
- Entry: on the **body** (preferred) or below the wick of the retrace candle.
- Works for entries and for management.

## 7. Arc vs double top (S2E3)
- Arc = price goes slightly **above** the double top / third touch, usually with corrective, low-momentum candles ("80/20").
- Do not discard the setup when price slightly exceeds the double top. The pattern often evolves into a deeper ascending channel before dropping.
- Entry: on the clear break of the trendline (1-2-3 line) after the arc, ideally with a retrace candle.

## 8. Stops and targets
- Stop above the top of the pattern or correction: typically **30-35 pips**; 25 pips seen on tighter setups.
- Avoid forcing trades needing 60-80 pip stops (rarely above 55-60). Wait for a tighter entry.
- Do not tighten the stop just for reward-to-risk; give room (S2E1).
- Target: previous low or pattern/trendline intersection on the higher timeframe. **[OPEN]** exact target rule.

## 9. Management
- Retrace candle on the 1H as the exit/lock-in signal: move the stop to just below the retrace (long) and let the market decide (S2E2).
- Move to break even once the move is established; small break-even or tiny losses are accepted.

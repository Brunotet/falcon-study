# Falcon strategy: rulebook, part 4 (full reads of S3E1 and S1E3)

Adds to falcon.md, part2 and part3. **[CODE]** = precise enough for the scanner. **[OPEN]** = vague.

## 20. The 15-minute chart (S3E1, "evolution of Falcon")
- Use the 15m **mainly in the impulsive phase** (about 95% of the time), at the higher-timeframe structure, not in the corrective phase. In a corrective phase it tags you in and out. Exception: refining a short-term entry inside a correction, only for experienced traders.
- **Four-point checklist [CODE]:**
  1. Impulsive phase (the bigger leg, not a short-term impulse).
  2. Higher-timeframe structure supports the move.
  3. **Minimum six 15m candles** of correction before placing the order.
  4. **As early in the impulsive move as possible** (the first corrections after the first impulse).
- Uses: (a) precise timing, (b) scaling in more often, (c) filtering an unclear 1H chart, (d) better management (tighter angle, cushion above the spike so break even is not hit), (e) **extra entries the 1H does not show** when momentum is strong and the 1H never gets a correction.
- **Stop: minimum 15 pips.** Typical 15m stops: 20 (risk entry at the top, reduced-risk flag), 30-35 on a pennant/larger flag, 40 conservative. Reward to risk roughly doubles versus the 1H entry.
- Entry shapes seen on the 15m: arc/double top inside an ascending channel, scoop, mini double top, pennant (tighter flag), hover on the trendline (6-8 candles), head and shoulders at the top (entry on the 15m candle close after the mini double top).
- Forecast all possible candle closes and structure first; always keep the 1H and higher timeframe as the main view.
- Instructor's caution: backtest at least 3 months before using it; trade the first 3-5 on demo or a fraction of the usual risk; be in Falcon at least 3 months first; do not overtrade (not 5-10 trades a day).
- Backtest implication: a faithful test needs 15m candles and must label phase (impulsive vs corrective) on the daily/4H. Stage-1 1H-only scanners miss his "extra entries".

## 21. Multi-touch confirmation (S1E3)
- Always "build a portfolio of reasons". Order: pattern within pattern, then multi-touch (1-2-3), then double top/bottom, then candle override.
- Minimum **two touches**; three is highest probability. A **second touch is tradable** (risk entry) because structures can break and go. With break even this is cheap: if it comes back for the third touch you were stopped at break even, and the pattern's probability just went up, so re-enter.
- Works on reversals and continuations, every timeframe. Check the same 1-2-3 on 4H, daily and 1H.
- Candles are secondary. If the move into the area is not corrective, you need a contingency plan (override with a candle shape, retrace candle, evening star).
- **[CODE]** score: touches count (2 = valid, 3 = high), multi-timeframe agreement of the same touches, nested pattern.

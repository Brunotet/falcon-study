# Falcon rulebook v7 (merged: falcon.md v6 + parts 2-6)

Source: the instructor's 34 lessons. Sections 1-9 are what he said. Section 10 is the scanner spec: items marked **[INSTRUCTOR]** are his numbers; items marked **[PROPOSED]** are my relative definitions (tunable) because he gives no fixed numbers and the market differs per flag. Nothing here is trading advice.

## 1. Core principles
- Trendline/pattern trading on price structure, no indicators. Nature of the market first, structure second, candles last (override only).
- Edge = mass psychology: price breaks a prior high/low, traps people on the wrong side, returns into the pattern. Think probable vs possible, never right/wrong. Only two trade grades: high probability and valid.
- Evaluate over a large sample (quarterly, ~30+ trades); order of wins/losses is unknowable.

## 2. Workflow and timeframes
- Weekly/daily/4H: outer structure and phase. 1H: setups, entries, management. 15m: refinement only (section 6.6). Always zoom out before a trade.
- Weekly top six pairs (sometimes ten); 2-3 trades a week; analysis Fri/Sat/Sun to stay neutral. Forecast several scenarios in advance, each with its entry type (probable and possible); re-forecast each evening.
- 8 major currencies crossed into pairs. Entry (stop) orders, not market orders.

## 3. Nature and phase
- Only impulsive (momentum) or corrective (low momentum, pattern-shaped). Impulses contain corrections and vice versa; a big impulse plus pattern = pole and flag.
- **80/20 rule:** if about 80% of a move is corrective candles, it is a correction, even with one impulsive candle. Next trade is the reversal.
- You usually know it is corrective only about halfway in (it retraces almost to the origin). After a corrective move, the next is impulsive.
- Impulsive phase = higher probability, the first correction after the first impulse is the best flag. Corrective phase = same-looking setups are lower probability; step back, do not force trades.

## 4. Structure
- Trendline through the **two most recent significant touches**, drawn on wicks. Min 2 touches (second is tradable), 3 = highest. A touch needs price to move off it.
- Pattern separation: when a line breaks, ask how. A corrective break means the old structure still rules; rein the line in to the latest two touches and see if a new pattern forms. Do not marry a line; wait for confirmation before evolving it.
- **Pattern within pattern:** same pattern nested in the same pattern (ascending in ascending = his number one go-to, descending in descending, bear flag in bear flag) raises probability. Ascending channel = reversal down; descending = reversal up.
- Outer structure first, then inner. Do not use years-old lines; recent price structure beats old support/resistance.
- Preferred: ascending/descending channels, double top/bottom inside a pattern, impulse-correction-continuation. Weak: big sideways/descending trickle, continuation with a big scoop/gap.
- **Arc:** price slightly above a double top/third touch with low-momentum candles, then drops. Slightly exceeding the top does not invalidate.
- **Running channel:** looks like a reversal channel but ends in a correction pattern the other way, signalling continuation; usually at structure top/bottom and aligned with the higher timeframe; ask "what is at the end, where are we in the structure". **Middle section:** impulse, correction, continuation inside 1-2-3; if it fails to reach the third touch, the move goes early.
- **Mass-psychology break:** a corrective (wedge/channel) break of a high/low is likely a trap; an impulsive break activates the 90% rule. Check if the same repeat occurred earlier.
- **90% rule:** after an **impulsive** break of a structure (daily/weekly), ~90% chance price fills ~90% of the correction, i.e. reaches the start of the correction (often 100%). Minimum target. If it stalls short, use pattern separation.

## 5. Trade quality
- Valid structure is not a valid trade; needs a valid area (outer structure, double top, higher-timeframe support).
- **High probability:** top/bottom of the pattern or outer structure, completion/edge, aligned with the higher timeframe, ideally after the prior high is broken. **Valid:** good structure but not fully supported. Target ratio **80% high probability / 20% valid**. If not supported, wait for the first impulse and take the next correction.
- No FOMO entries; skip what you cannot monitor; discretion about 5-10%.

## 6. Entries and setups
### 6.1 Risk vs reduced risk
- Risk entry: inside the pattern, unconfirmed, tighter stop (~15-30), roughly double reward to risk. Reduced risk: on the break of a flag/pattern, larger stop, less reward. Both risk 1%.
- On a very large pattern do not use a break entry (100-200 pip stops); use the nested smaller flag/channel.
### 6.2 Flags (mostly bear flags; bull = mirror)
- **Tight/first flag (highest probability):** flat correction after the first impulse, **minimum 6-8 one-hour candles** (can be 12-14), entry order a couple of pips beyond the extreme, stop beyond the other side (~20-30). Reduced risk. First flag at the top/bottom of a structure is the best; wait for the first impulse rather than calling the top.
- Steep flag: avoid (tags you in and out) unless there is something to retest. Larger flag after the tight one: risk entry at the second top/bottom (double top inside), stop ~30-40. Running channel (above), Falcon flag (6.5).
- Trap pattern: spike, full retrace, then a flag the other way: take that flag.
### 6.3 Other entry shapes
- **1H retrace candle:** candle retraces more than 50% of the impulse candle (need not be next); entry on the body or below the wick; stop ~25; target at least the 90% rule. Also usable to lock profit.
- **Hover:** tight flag along the trendline near the top or the break of an ascending channel, 6-8 candles, reduced risk, stop 25-30.
- **Third-touch overrides** (third touch not needed): double top/bottom inside the pattern; 6-8 candle flag after the second-touch impulse; retrace candle; evening/morning star, doji, hanging man at the top (bonus only).
### 6.4 The ten go-to setups (all at structure top/bottom, entry on 1H, optional 15m)
1 risk entry top, three-touch channel with retrace candle; 2 hover at top/break of ascending channel; 3 bull flag third touch (double bottom override), stop ~30; 4 double top inside ascending channel at structure top; 5 double bottom inside descending channel at bottom; 6 ascending within ascending at the top (stop 30-40); 7 reduced-risk tight flag after the first impulse (stop ~25); 8 15m refinement of a 1H flag (>=6 fifteen-minute candles, stop 20-25); 9 daily-structure completion via a 15m 1-2-3 scoop; 10 Falcon flag.
### 6.5 Falcon flag
- Flag taken, price retraces to entry, break even; then a larger version forms (at least a double top, sometimes triple/quad) and breaks; re-enter at the top of the larger one. Mostly sells; often seen on Euro-Aussie and Aussie.
### 6.6 15m chart
- Mainly the impulsive phase (~95%), higher-timeframe structure supporting, **>=6 fifteen-minute candles** before the order, as early in the move as possible. Stop minimum 15 pips (typically 20; 30-35 for pennants). Extra entries the 1H does not show. Backtest 3 months before using; not for beginners.
### 6.7 Candles
- Secondary to structure; do not skip a good structure for a wrong-looking candle.

## 7. Orders, stops, targets, risk
- Entry (stop) order just beyond the trigger, no expiry, cancel manually. No limit/market orders. Forecast, checklist, then place.
- Stop beyond the pattern/correction top: typically 25-35, tight setups 15-25, **minimum 15**, rarely above 50-60 (skip 60-80 setups). Do not tighten just to improve reward to risk; do not halve the stop to hit a number.
- Risk 1% per position; max about 2% total open risk (3% for some); correlated trades count together. Beginners: first trade to break even before a second. To scale in, the original must be at break even.
- Minimum about **3:1** reward to risk, uncapped. Targets: start of the correction (90% rule) minimum, impulse length for flags, prior high/low or trendline on the higher timeframe. Usually no TP; use the stop and structure. Optional TP only when unattended.
- Position size = 1% / (stop pips x pip value).

## 8. Management
- **A. Reduced-risk flag mid-structure:** after the impulse closes solidly, break even at roughly 1-1.3%; expect 1-3 re-entries.
- **B. High-probability trade at the top/bottom of a structure:** be lenient, no early break even even at ~1%+; wait for a significant impulsive close/break of the structure; use candle closes, not wicks. After a retrace candle following a break, lock a little profit.
- Locking: move the stop to the broken level (line in the sand). Re-enter after a tag-out.
- Long holds: accept 10-15% pullbacks on 30-60% targets; keep a runner.
- A 1% loss on a plan-compliant trade is not a mistake. Holding over weekends is fine.

## 9. Probability and evaluation
- Coin theory: weighted edge. A 3:1 month with 3 wins, 5 losses, 2 break evens is still +4%. Judge quarterly. Count wins, losses, break evens, missed trades. Avoid over-trading, revenge trades, banking at 1%, forcing month-end trades. Step back in corrective periods.

## 10. Scanner spec (for backtests)
### 10.1 Pipeline
1. Daily/4H: label phase (impulsive vs corrective, 80/20) and find outer structure (channel lines from the two latest touches, prior swing high/low).
2. Locate: price within the top (or bottom) zone of the outer structure; score nesting (same pattern inside), touches (2/3), multi-timeframe agreement.
3. 1H: find the first impulse after a structure break/rejection, then the first correction; entry order beyond the flag extreme after the minimum candle count; stop beyond the other extreme (min 15 pips).
4. Optional 15m refinement in the impulsive phase only.
5. Target at least the 90% rule point if an impulsive break occurred, else the structure low/high; keep the 3:1 minimum filter.
6. Manage with rule 8A/8B; record win/loss/break-even separately.
### 10.2 Relative definitions (flags and impulses vary, so define by ratios to the same chart)
- **[INSTRUCTOR]** flag minimum 6-8 one-hour candles (6 on the 15m), stop min 15 pips, ~80/20 corrective share, retrace candle >50% of the impulse candle, 2% total open risk, 1% per trade, minimum 3:1.
- **[PROPOSED] Impulsive leg:** consecutive candles in one direction where the net move is at least about 2x the median net move of the last 3 legs, or its candles' mean true range exceeds the 20-bar mean. Treat as tunable.
- **[PROPOSED] Corrective share:** share of candles in a leg whose range is below the mean range of the preceding impulsive leg; corrective if >= about 0.8.
- **[PROPOSED] Tight flag:** at least 6 candles, flag high-to-low range at most about 35-50% of the impulse leg it follows, no single candle larger than the impulse's mean candle, slope shallow relative to the impulse. Tightness is thus relative to that flag's own impulse, not a pip number.
- **[PROPOSED] Top/bottom zone:** within roughly 15-20% of the outer structure's height from its upper (lower) line or the prior swing extreme.
- **[PROPOSED] Impulsive break:** the breaking candle or two-candle close beyond the line exceeds about 1.5x mean true range and closes beyond the line.
- All [PROPOSED] constants are starting points to tune on a held-out period, not instructor rules.
### 10.3 Test discipline
- Report trades per setup, win/loss/break-even counts, average R, and results by quarter. Under about 30 trades per setup is noise. Compare each filter (nesting, location, first-flag, 15m) against the plain scanner.
- Deriv synthetics are random and cannot validate psychology; use forex/indices.
